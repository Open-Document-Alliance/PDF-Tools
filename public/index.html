PDF Tools Remote MCP

An MCP endpoint for reading, filling, signing and flattening PDF forms. It is meant for AI agents, not for browsing.

Endpoint: /mcp, Streamable HTTP, no authentication.

Nothing is stored. Each call carries its own document, the work happens in memory, and the result comes back in the response.

Open source, MIT licensed, by Open Document Alliance. Source and documentation: github.com/Open-Document-Alliance/PDF-Tools. Privacy and terms.
